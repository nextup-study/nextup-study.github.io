How it picks what's next

% done is weighted by hours, so finishing an 8 h render moves the bar more than a 1 h caption.

Work time is counted day by day until the deadline, using your timetable: days with class get your class-day hours, free days get your free-day hours.

Slack = that work time minus the hours still left. Lowest slack goes first.

Close-it-out rule: if a project is 80%+ done with 2 h or less left, and finishing it won't put your most urgent project behind, it jumps to the top.

Status: On track using under 70% of your time, Tight 70–100%, Behind more work than time.
